# Atomic reconstruction evaluation

The frozen evaluation contains 28 reserved parents and 1,568 cases. Original, translated and two displaced variants use two five-camera sets and Gaussian noise levels 0, 0.003 and 0.01 Å. There are 1,120 Gaussian cases and 448 cases with an additional 0.10 Å offset to one centroid.

Coordinates are scored by species-constrained Cartesian matching. Stable class-changing pairs require agreement at detector tolerances 0.005, 0.01 and 0.02 Å. The paired one-SE selector is primary; fixed-four, minimum-loss, null-guard, observation-only, physical-only and Huber results provide comparisons. Bootstrap resampling groups all cases by parent.

Exact inputs, exclusions, seeds and selection records are in [the atomic results](../results/symmetry_selector_refinement/). Tables 1 and S1–S3 report this evaluation.
