\section{Conclusion}
When a rendering is a known projection of a known object, the information the rendering carries is
computable without a model, and that number turns a benchmark into an attribution instrument. Applied here
it contradicts the reading it was built to test: exact geometry leaves most models limited by the symbolic
residual rather than by perception, the bottleneck moves with model strength, and a learned extraction
stage can fabricate its output completely while remaining syntactically perfect. The method transfers to
any modality where the forward rendering is known and invertible.
