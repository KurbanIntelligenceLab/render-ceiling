\section*{Reproducibility statement}
The oracle, the label pipeline and both classical baselines are deterministic and CPU-only; the released
artifact contains the render protocol, the labelling tolerances and sweep, the exact feature specification
and seed for the cell-metric reference, the verbatim model prompts, and per-structure predictions for every
arm reported here, so each accuracy in this paper recomputes from released vectors without API access.
Every numeric claim was written by a script that reads the corresponding checkpoint record rather than
transcribed, and a verification gate refuses any manuscript sentence carrying a value absent from those
records or an accuracy without its sample and decode budget. Each analysis was pre-registered before its
numbers existed; the pre-registrations are released alongside the results, including for the analyses whose
outcome contradicted the registered expectation. Values that did not reproduce are documented as
non-recoveries (see Limitations). Dataset metadata is provided in Croissant format validated against the
reference library.

\section*{Ethics statement}
This work involves no human subjects and no personally identifying data. Recruitment of qualified
crystallographers for a human baseline was attempted and failed, so no human-subjects protocol was
executed and no human-performance claim is made. The structures are drawn from a public
crystallographic database under its stated licence, and every render, label and prediction vector is
released with the paper. The models evaluated are third-party systems accessed through their public
interfaces under their terms of service; we report their identifiers and decode budgets rather than
anonymising them, since the accuracy claims are only interpretable against a named arm. We see one
foreseeable misuse: an oracle that recovers structure from a render measures information, not
scientific competence, and a ceiling reported here should not be read as evidence that a model, or a
person, can solve the underlying task. The withdrawn claims and non-recoveries are published
alongside the surviving ones for that reason.

\section*{Statement on the use of AI tools}
This work was carried out with substantial use of an AI research assistant; this section is the disclosure,
and per the author guidelines it does not count against the page limit.

\textbf{Categories that apply.} The assistant participated in proposing and refining hypotheses (the
attribution-ladder design, the cue-sufficiency partition, the visibility conditions); designing and giving
feedback on methodology and experiments (every pre-registration in the released ledger was drafted by it
before the corresponding numbers existed); implementing methods (the render pipeline, the oracle, the label
pipeline, every evaluation harness, every figure script); cleaning and reformatting datasets (retrieval,
composition exclusion, the symmetry-tolerance quarantine); and interpreting results. Of the recommended
categories it also drafted parts of this paper, created the figures, and performed literature search.

\textbf{What is mechanically checked rather than asserted.} Every numeric claim here is read from a
checkpoint record by script; a build gate refuses any sentence carrying a value absent from those records,
or an accuracy stated without its sample and decode budget. All six figures regenerate from their
checkpoint records and are verified byte-for-byte. A separate linter checks the source for unescaped
percent signs, unbalanced math, and undefined references. Each check was itself tested against a deliberate
violation of the rule it enforces.

\textbf{Errors it introduced, and how they were caught.} We report these because the failure modes are the
useful part. First, a published minimum-detectable-difference of 0.162 for the render-convention null was
computed with an unpaired two-proportion approximation carrying no power term, for a test that was paired.
Recomputing per comparison showed significance was unreachable in 7 of 16 comparisons at any outcome, which
made the null weaker than the retracted figure implied, and the contribution was demoted accordingly.
Second, a figure plotted parameter counts for models whose sizes are undisclosed; the axis was deleted and
undisclosed-size models are now barred from any parameter axis. Third, a zero-shot sweep ran twice and an
aggregation step silently mixed both runs; the leaderboard was rebuilt from single-run per-structure
vectors and the second run is published as a labelled replication whose run-to-run spread is reported as a
property of the benchmark. Further withdrawals, including three documented non-recoveries, are verbatim in
the supplementary's retraction record.

\textbf{What a human is accountable for.} The authors take responsibility for every claim in this paper,
including those produced or checked by the assistant, and for the decision about which results ship and
which are reported as withdrawn.
